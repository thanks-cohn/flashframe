# Quick Actions visibility smoke checklist

1. Open two images.
2. Select image A and confirm Quick Actions are visible.
3. Click the Quick Actions `×`; confirm the toolbar hides while image A remains selected.
4. Right-click image A and choose **Show Quick Actions**; confirm the toolbar returns.
5. Right-click image A again and choose **Hide Quick Actions**; confirm it hides.
6. Select image B; confirm its Quick Actions remain visible and image A's preference did not affect it.
7. Multi-select A and B; confirm the toolbar is available unless Quick Actions are hidden for every selected image.
8. Export/save an FCX snapshot, reopen it, select image A, and confirm its hidden state is preserved.
9. Right-click near viewport edges and confirm the context menu stays on-screen.
10. Verify Escape, outside click, scroll, resize, and window blur dismiss the context menu.
